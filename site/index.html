Capabilities

Nodal analysis

Match inflow performance against tubing performance to find the operating point, and see how it moves as reservoir pressure declines.

Multiphase flow

Pressure traverse calculations for oil, gas, and water through tubing and flowlines, with selectable correlations for your fluid system.

Artificial lift design

Evaluate gas lift, ESP, and rod pump scenarios side by side to see which sustains production economically as the well ages.

Sensitivity analysis

Sweep tubing size, wellhead pressure, water cut, or skin, and quantify what each one actually costs you in rate.

Production forecasting

Project rates and cumulative recovery over time to support reserves work and development planning.

Reporting

Export results, curves, and case comparisons in a form you can hand straight to a partner or an approval committee.

About

WellsSim is built for production and reservoir engineers who need dependable answers about well behaviour without wrestling the software to get them.

The aim is a tool that is quick enough for a screening study in the morning and rigorous enough for the case you defend in the afternoon.
